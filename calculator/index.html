How we calculated this

  • Your guest count and the share of guests who will be drinking
  • How many hours the bar is open, at about two drinks in the first hour and one per hour after that
  • Your drinking profile, which scales the total up or down
  • Your bar style, which sets the beer, wine and spirit split

Every event is different. These estimates are designed to give you a strong starting point and help prevent running short or significantly overbuying. Bottle counts are always rounded up.
